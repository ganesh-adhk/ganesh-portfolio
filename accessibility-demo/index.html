Reading-friendly mode, on demand.

This page watches for signs that the visual layer is getting in the way — frantic mouse movement, scrolling back-and-forth, hovering the same paragraph repeatedly — and offers to turn the noise down. You can enable it manually any time with the button below.

Try the heuristics

Wiggle your mouse rapidly without clicking anything, or scroll up and down quickly, or hover this paragraph several times in a row. After about a second the prompt will appear in the corner — or nearer the element it thinks you were struggling with.

How it integrates

Components opt in by adding data-accessible="text" or data-accessible="surface". Three.js scenes register a callback so they can throttle particles, slow animations, or switch to a simpler shader when the calm view turns on.
