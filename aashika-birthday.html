१ Birthday Wish

To my dear sister, on your special day

Aashika, today the candles are lit for you. I still remember the little sister who used to follow me around the house asking endless questions — and now look at you, growing into someone I admire more with every passing year. Your laughter still fills our home the same way it always has.

May this new year of your life be wrapped in everything good: warm mornings, easy laughter, brave choices, and people who love you the way our family does. Whatever you reach for next, I'll be right behind you, cheering louder than anyone.

Happy Birthday, Bhai-ko-pyaari-bahini!

And congratulations on your A Grade

Your result in the +2 NEB examinations didn't surprise me one bit — I've watched you study late into the night and ask one more question than anyone else in the room. That A grade is the proof of every quiet hour you put in. We are all so proud of you.
